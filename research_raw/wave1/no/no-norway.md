## Top themes (ranked by evidence in pages read)
- National frameworks for requirements: POFIN 1.0, openBIM Harmony, ISO 19650 terms (EIR, BEP, LOIN, CDE).
- MMI (modellmodenhetsindeks) coding of objects, shared by Statsbygg, Nye Veier, Bane NOR, Statens vegvesen.
- IFC versions: IFC2x3 vs IFC4 vs IFC4.3 (infra), outlook IFC4.4 and IFC5.
- IFC for infrastructure: roads, rail, VA (water and wastewater) detail models in IFC 4.3.
- Machine-checkable requirements: IDS, IFC Validation Service, Solibri-style rule checks.
- NS 8360 property sets (Milestone, IsProcured, DuplicateOwnedBy), process status codes.
- Rebar (armering) from open models to ordering and production.
- BCF and openCDE for coordination and CDE exchange.
- bSDD and classification (SN/K 518, NS 3451 typical in practice).
- FDV (operation and maintenance) handover from models.
- Standardisation governance: Standard Norge SN/K 257, CEN TC442.

## What people want (the goals behind the questions)
- Know exactly what the client (Statsbygg, Nye Veier, Bane NOR) requires in a delivery and be able to prove compliance automatically.
- Get Revit exports accepted: right MVD, mapping, property sets, MMI values.
- Check models for missing data (MMI, fire class, classification, FDV) and list failures by discipline.
- Georeferenced infrastructure models in Norwegian coordinate systems (EUREF89 NTM).
- Quantities and room lists for cost and FM.
- Understand the alphabet soup (IDS, BCF, bSDD, LOIN, MMI).

## How people write here
Inferred from official wording and from the synthetic style, not from forum posts, so treat as low confidence.
- Register: professional bokmål, short and practical, imperative or short question ("sjekk...", "lag...").
- Heavy code-switching: IFC, Pset, proxy, Solibri, Revit, BCF, clash kept in English.
- Abbreviations as everyday vocabulary: RIB, RIV, ARK (disciplines), FDV, BTA/NTA, TEK17, MMI.
- Norwegian disciplines/terms: bestiller (client), leverandør, kum, armering, romliste, etasje.
- Illustrative fragments: "hvilke kategorier kommer de fra"; "mangler MMI i modellen"; "lag BCF av de over 50 mm".
- Typical: lowercase start, no greeting, missing file/version context, mild frustration ("??").
- Risk: æøå encoding in names and properties after export.
