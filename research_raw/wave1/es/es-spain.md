# España: foros y blogs: exportar IFC, problemas, Plan BIM en la contratación pública

Cell: es-spain · Languages: Spanish (plus English threads from Autodesk forums) · Collected: 2026-11-06 · Records: 30 real / 17 synthetic · Communities covered: Autodesk forums (ES thread plus EN threads), Espacio BIM, e-zigurat, BIMnD, Arkance, Graphisoft Community, construible/esmartcity/interempresas news, Hacienda (Junta Consultiva), EU public buyers community

Caveat: the WebSearch budget ran out after about 8 searches, so breadth is below target. Only one genuine Spanish-language forum question was found (snippet only). Most "real" records are Autodesk English threads, Spanish explainers and Plan BIM news. Treat the Spanish user voice as under-sampled.

## Top themes (ranked by frequency)
- IFC class mapping: families exported as IfcBuildingElementProxy or "Not exported".
- IFCExportAs / IFCExportType shared parameters not taking effect.
- Parameters and Psets missing after export (type vs instance, pset mapping txt, unit mismatch).
- Revit 2027 new Parameter Mapping UI replacing pset.txt, with gaps.
- Coordinates and origin lost or misplaced when opening/linking IFC in Revit.
- Information loss in Revit to Archicad (or other tool) IFC exchange.
- Structural elements (steel connections, parts) missing in export.
- Plan BIM in public procurement: levels, thresholds, timetable (April 2024 to 2030).
- Free viewers and tools (BIMvision, CYPE IFC Builder) for checking IFC.
- Training and grants tied to the Plan BIM.

## What people want
- Make Revit categories and generic families export as the correct IFC class instead of proxy.
- Keep custom shared parameters as Psets in the IFC.
- Keep positions and shared coordinates across tools.
- Know what the public tender (pliego) demands in IFC and BIM level, and how to comply.
- Check an IFC quickly in a free viewer.

## How people write here
- Spanish forum posts are short and context-poor: no Revit or IFC version, no steps (e.g. "problemas con los ifc").
- English-style plural apostrophe "IFC's" appears; clipped statement-of-symptom titles.
- Code-switching: IFC class and parameter names kept verbatim in English (IfcBuildingElementProxy, IFCExportAs, Pset, proxy).
- Professional-blog register is formal ("cabe destacar", "se deben realizar pruebas").
- Likely lowercase chat style with missing accents and slang ("proxys", "ifc", "pset", "lejisimos") when typed to an AI.
- Fragments: "se pierden las coordenadas", "no se exportan los parametros", "sale como proxy".

## Local ecosystem
- Comisión Interministerial BIM (CIBIM), Ministerio de Transportes: https://cibim.transportes.gob.es/ (formerly cibim.mitma.es). Plan BIM approved by the Consejo de Ministros on 27 June 2023; rollout from April 2024 for high-value contracts (reported threshold 5,538,000 EUR) to 2030; five BIM levels.
- Junta Consultiva de Contratación Pública del Estado (Hacienda): report on the plan.
- Standards: UNE-EN ISO 16739 (IFC), cited in an EU summary of the Plan BIM.
- Software: CYPE (IFC Builder, BIMserver.center), Datacomp BIMvision, Archicad, Allplan, Tricalc, Solibri.
- Educators/portals: Espacio BIM, e-zigurat, BIMnD, Arkance, construible.es.
