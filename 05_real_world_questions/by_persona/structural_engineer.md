# Structural engineer: who they are and what they do with IFC

> Persona tag in the data: `structural_engineer`. Evidence base: `kb.py stats --persona structural_engineer` gives 187 records (111 real, 76 synthetic) from `research_raw/wave1`. Last compiled 2026-11-06.
> Sibling pages: [MEP engineer](mep_engineer.md), [civil / infrastructure engineer](civil_infra_engineer.md), [estimator / QS](estimator_qs.md), [developer](developer_programmer.md). Topic overview: [MEP, structural, rebar](../by_topic/mep_structural_rebar.md).

## In plain words

A structural engineer designs and checks the part of a building that holds it up: foundations, columns, beams, slabs, shear walls, steel connections and the reinforcing bars (rebar) inside concrete. In daily life they live in two kinds of programs:

1. **Calculation programs** (ETABS, Robot, SCAD / Lira, STAAD, RFEM, SAP2000, SCIA, PKPM). These work with a *stick model*: lines for beams and columns, points for joints, supports and loads. They do not care about what a beam looks like.
2. **Modelling / detailing programs** (Revit Structure, Tekla Structures, Allplan, Advance Steel, Archicad, Bocad). These work with real 3D bodies, bolts, welds and individual rebar bars.

IFC is what they are told to use when the client or the architect asks for "the IFC of the structure". Their typical feeling is: "I model carefully, press Export, and the other side sees axes only, or no steel connections, or a thousand grey boxes." Almost every complaint is about what **gets lost between two programs**, not about IFC theory. In the data this is reinforced by the fact that the *same* few threads (Tekla to Revit, Revit to a calculation program, Revit steel connections) are asked again and again in English, Russian, Chinese and Spanish.

What an IFC-aware assistant can realistically do for them: look into an IFC and tell, in plain words, "this file has solid beams but no calculation model", "these 40 columns have no material", "your rebar arrived as IfcReinforcingBar, not proxy", "here are the quantities, and here is where the numbers are missing".

## Goals

- **Get through the export step without losing things.** Beams, columns, connections, bolts, rebar should arrive as the right kind of object, in the right place (`en-struct-r022`, `en-struct-r024`, `ru-dwg-other-r001`).
- **Hand off a stick model that a solver accepts**, or at least know exactly what is missing (supports, loads, density, units) before pressing send (`en-eng-r002`, `en-struct-s004`, `it-struct-s017`).
- **Get honest quantities**: concrete volume by level, rebar kg per diameter, steel kg by profile (`en-eng-s005`, `ru-dwg-revit-s005`, `de-struct-s012`).
- **Meet the client's "IFC delivery" clause** without learning the schema: "the customer demands IFC and Revit only shows axes" (`ru-dwg-other-r002`).
- **Keep data attached to the objects**: concrete grade, fire rating, user parameters survive the trip (`en-tekla-r015`, `en-tekla-s013`, `en-ghrevitifc-r028`).

## Vocabulary they use (and what they do not know)

Words in the first column are how the persona actually writes (often in the local language, often with English tool names inside).

| They say | Meaning for them | What they usually do not know / confuse |
|---|---|---|
| КЖ, КМ, расчётная схема, узлы, стержни, оси (ru); 结构模型, 钢筋, 节点 (zh); estructura, pilares, vigas (es); 鉄骨, 梁, 柱 (ja) | Reinforced-concrete drawing set, steel detailing, the analysis scheme with bars and nodes | The IFC class names (`IfcBeam`, `IfcColumn`, `IfcMember`, `IfcStructuralCurveMember`). They only know the names inside their program. |
| "обобщённые / прокси" (generic / proxy) | A column came in as a generic object | Why: the exporter had no class for it. They do not know `IfcExportAs`, or that "proxy" is `IfcBuildingElementProxy`. |
| "Reference view", "Design Transfer", "Coordination view" | Menu items in the exporter | What the difference is; they pick by habit or by what the coordinator asked. |
| "арматура / 钢筋 / rebar / armadura" | Bars with diameter and length; sometimes only a weight table | That bars in IFC are separate objects (`IfcReinforcingBar`) or may be absent; that "cover" and "spacing" live in properties, not in the bar itself (`en-eng-r010`). |
| "analytical model", "расчётная модель" | The stick model for the solver | That it is a *separate branch* in IFC (`IfcStructuralAnalysisModel`) which most viewers do not show (`en-eng-r001`). |
| "класс бетона B30, C30/37, C50/60", "S275" | Material grade | That the grade is just a text property in IFC; nothing checks that it is consistent. |
| "узел", "connection", "weld", "bolt" | Steel joint parts | Whether the exporter includes them (a checkbox) and that they become fasteners/plates or disappear. |
| "Pset", "BaseQuantities" (sometimes) | Property group on an object | That quantities (Qto) and properties (Pset) are different; that missing quantities mean the exporter did not write them, not that the model is empty. |

Plain-language bridge for a non-BIM tester: an IFC file is a text database of "things" (a beam, a column, a level) and "relations" (this beam sits on this level, this beam has this material). The persona knows the **things** from daily work and almost never knows the **relations** or the class names. A good test prompt from this persona therefore names the physical thing and the symptom, not the IFC entity (see "How to role-play" below). Entities are explained in the [entities cheat sheet](../../01_ifc_basics/03_entities_cheat_sheet.md) and the structural/MEP part of the [domains overview](../../01_ifc_basics/08_domains_mep_structural_infra_4d5d_fm.md). Revit-side options: [Revit IFC export reference](../../03_software/revit/ifc_export_reference.md). Multilingual terms: [glossary](../../08_glossary/glossary_multilingual.md).

## How to role-play this persona in tests

**Voice.** A busy practitioner with a deadline, not a student. Short, technical, impatient. They name the physical object ("колонны", "beam", "Stahlliste", "钢筋") and the program ("Тексла", "Robot", "ETABS"), not the IFC class. They often write lower-case, drop punctuation, mix local language with English tool names, and use slang ("ифс", "ревит", "текла", "pls"). Typical register in Russian: *"глянь файл из Теклы, в Ревите одни оси"* (`ru-dwg-other-s002`); in English: *"we lost beams and columns in the IFC after the exporter update"* (`en-struct-s002`); in Chinese: *"帮我查一下模型里哪些钢筋没有直径参数"* (`zh-eng-s011`).

**Typical context.** One IFC at hand (from Tekla, Revit, Allplan or a calculation program), a meeting or a client request tomorrow, and a specific downstream target (Robot, ETABS, SCAD, a contractor, the BIM coordinator's Navisworks). They rarely state the IFC version or export settings unless asked ("2x3 CV" is the exception, `de-dach-s013`). Real posts often attach a file or screenshot instead of describing; an MCP tester should therefore assume `needs_file = true` for most prompts.

**Knowledge level.**
- Strong: structural behaviour, materials, rebar detailing, steel profiles, codes (Eurocodes, national codes), their own program.
- Weak: IFC entity names, relationship logic, why a viewer shows only axes, the difference between physical and analytical model in IFC terms, export "views".
- Believes: IFC should be a perfect copy of the native model (the root of pains 1 and 2). Will say "в Ревите есть, в IFC нет" (it is in Revit, not in the IFC) as proof of a bug.
- Trusts numbers from the model and wants to know the **source** and **unit** of every figure (kg, m3, mm).

**Do / do not in a test.**
- Do ask for lists and counts per level, per section size, per concrete class; do ask for "what is missing" and "what do I need before sending to the solver".
- Do include a deliberate trap now and then: the model has no analytical branch; rebar was never exported; a property is text ("C30" vs a number); units are mm vs m. A good assistant says so instead of inventing values.
- Do not give the persona perfect IFC vocabulary. Prompts naming `IfcStructuralCurveMember` appear only in the two or three "expert" variants (`en-struct-s004`).
- Do not ask this persona to write IfcOpenShell code; that is the [developer](developer_programmer.md).

**Typical follow-ups (to continue a conversation).**
1. "А почему так? Это настройки экспорта или баг?" / "is this my export setting or a bug in the exporter?"
2. "Which version of the exporter / IFC should I use instead?" (expects 2x3 vs 4, reference vs design transfer, explained in plain words)
3. "Покажи только колонны 2-го этажа" / "now only level 2" (narrowing by level)
4. "Save the fixed file as a new one, don't touch the original." (expects a non-destructive write)
5. "Make a table I can paste in Excel." / "в эксель выгрузи"
6. "Compare with the previous revision, tell me what moved." (see `no-norway-s009`, `it-roundtrip-s016`)
7. Frustration: "I already tried that, still axes only" (expects a new idea, not a repeat).

**Quick judge checklist for answers to this persona.**
- Does the answer distinguish *physical* elements from the *analysis* model, and say plainly if one of them is absent?
- Are counts given per level / per type, and do they match the file (not guessed)?
- Are units stated, and is "no data" reported as "no data" rather than 0?
- When the fix is outside the file (exporter option, program setting), does the answer say which program and which setting, without claiming certainty about a version it was not shown?
