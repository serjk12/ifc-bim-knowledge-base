# Geometry quality, openings/voids, curtain walls, stairs and railings

Topics covered (vocabulary): `geometry_quality`, `openings_voids`, `curtain_walls`, `stairs_railings`.
Evidence base: 335 real records (275 distinct source pages) and 115 synthetic prompts carrying at least one of these four topic tags. Language of the real records: en 307, zh 13, ru 7, ja 5, de 2, es 1. Per topic: geometry_quality 232 real, openings_voids 106, curtain_walls 21, stairs_railings only 8 (the stairs and railings area is thin; claims about it are lighter than elsewhere). There are no real records in Korean, French, Italian or Portuguese on these topics; prompts in those languages below are synthetic. Source mix of the real records: 233 of 335 are GitHub issues (Autodesk revit-ifc, IfcOpenShell/Bonsai, That Open web-ifc, xBIM), 72 are forum threads, 23 blog posts; the vocabulary is therefore developer-flavoured.

## In plain words

IFC does not store a wall as a picture of a wall. It stores a *recipe* for a shape (an extruded outline, a block of triangles, a free-form solid) plus separate objects for holes, hosts and parts. If any piece of that recipe is missing, unsupported, tiny-off or just too heavy, the viewer shows a missing wall, a window floating in front of a solid wall, a pile of triangles that freezes the laptop, or an element in the wrong place. Three ideas explain most of the questions below:

1. **Holes are separate objects.** A door or window sits in an *opening* object (`IfcOpeningElement`) that is linked to the wall by a "voids" relationship and to the door by a "fills" relationship. The wall's own shape has no hole until a program subtracts the opening. Programs differ in how well they do that. See [Geometry, placement, coordinates, georeferencing and units](../../01_ifc_basics/06_geometry_placement_coordinates_units.md) sections 1.5-1.9.
2. **A shape can be written in several ways, and not every program reads every way.** Parametric (extrusion), mesh (triangulated faces), advanced boundary representations and mapped copies are all legal. Revit's own *Open IFC* and *Link IFC* paths turn imports into DirectShape objects that cannot be edited like native walls, see [Revit IFC import and link reference](../../03_software/revit/ifc_import_link_reference.md).
3. **Curtain walls, stairs and railings are assemblies.** A curtain wall should contain panels and mullions, a stair should contain flights, landings and railings. Exporters decide how much of the assembly is written, and tools differ in whether they follow the parts. See the entity list in [Entities cheat sheet](../../01_ifc_basics/03_entities_cheat_sheet.md) and the Revit class mapping in [Revit IFC export reference](../../03_software/revit/ifc_export_reference.md).

For an AI assistant connected to an IFC or Revit model, most of these requests are *diagnostic* ("which walls have a door but no hole?", "what makes this file 800 MB?", "do the curtain walls have mullions?") and the right answer is a list with GlobalIds plus a plain-language cause, not a guess about what the viewer "should" do. A few are *write* requests (add openings, fill stair attributes) that need confirmation.

**How people write about this.** Short, visual and symptom-first: "walls without holes", "windows float", "mullions missing", "nothing in 3D", "file does not open". Many posts name only the software and version ("Revit 2025", "exporter 25.4.x", "Bonsai 0.9", "web-ifc 0.0.68") and attach a screenshot or an IFC. Developers write in the vocabulary of the entities (`IfcRelVoidsElement`, `IfcSweptDiskSolid`), architects in the vocabulary of the tool ("join geometry", "Void Cut", "Building Element Proxy"). Chinese and Russian posts typically ask "Revit opens the IFC and elements are lost or deformed" or "which representation should I export for Navisworks". In German-speaking sources the word *Durchbruch/Aussparung/Öffnung* (penetration/recess/opening) appears next to the technical term, and the *ProvisionForVoid* convention (a proxy that requests a hole) is a recurring topic.

## Problems in depth

### 1. Openings missing, not cut, displaced; doors and windows outside the wall

**Symptoms in users' words.**
- (en) "Wall exported uncut with no IfcOpeningElement/IfcRelVoidsElement when beam top equals wall top; a 1 mm offset fixes it." [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/985)
- (en) "Shaft openings sharing an edge with floors are missing or the floor boundary is wrong in the IFC." [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/609)
- (en) "Opening an Archicad IFC in Revit 2025-2027 puts windows outside the wall face in 3D." [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/1019) (tracked separately from a 2D-symbol issue [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/1018))
- (en) "When I open IFC from Revit I get 'Opening Cuts Skipped'; Apply Openings fixes it." [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/2887/dynamic-csv-to-cost-schedule-now-available) (the advice in the thread: the "Void Limit" / max-voids-per-element setting, default 30, skips subtraction on elements with many openings [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/3754/opening-cuts-skipped-in-bonsai-studio-0-9); one reporter had 37 such elements, mostly walls and slabs [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/3754/opening-cuts-skipped-in-bonsai-studio-0-9))
- (en) "Walls show up without openings so windows are not visible; all elements are IfcBuildingElementProxy." [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/398)
- (de) the blog "IFC ProvisionForVoid in Revit - Öffnen vs. Referenzieren / Binden": openings from IFC do not cut the walls in Revit [src](https://bim-me-up.com/ifc-provisionforvoid-in-revit-offnen-vs-referenzieren-binden/)

**Why it happens.** An opening is a separate object, linked to the host by `IfcRelVoidsElement` and to the door/window by `IfcRelFillsElement`. The wall body itself has no hole; a viewer or script has to subtract the opening. So a hole can be missing because (a) the relationship is missing (an opening not related to a product is invalid and some tools hide it [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/7626)), (b) the tool skips the boolean (void limit, tolerance, kernel failure), (c) the exporter did not write the cut (flush beam, join geometry, shared edges, sketch-based openings), (d) the importer places the filling in the wrong host position (2D symbols/FootPrint in hosted families). See [06 doc, section 1.8](../../01_ifc_basics/06_geometry_placement_coordinates_units.md).

**What fixes it.** Check the three relationship counts before blaming the viewer; in viewers apply or raise the openings limit, or extract the heavy elements; on the Revit export side offset the flush element by 1 mm or change the join; on import use a Revit/add-in build in which the placement bug is fixed; in Revit use "link then bind/convert" only as a documented workaround (not a guaranteed fix). The Archicad-to-Revit window offset is a reported regression (absent in Revit 2024, present 2025-2027).

**A good assistant should:** (1) for every wall list `IfcRelVoidsElement` openings and `IfcRelFillsElement` fillings; (2) report walls that have a filling but no void, openings without a host and openings without a filling, with GlobalIds and storeys; (3) say whether the opening geometry and placement exist (placement relative to the host); (4) only then say "this looks like a viewer/limit problem" or "the file lacks the cut". **Clarifying questions:** Which viewer or software shows the problem? Does another viewer show the hole? IFC version/view definition (2x3 Coordination View, IFC4 Reference View, Design Transfer View)? Exporter or add-in build? Are the windows real `IfcWindow` objects or proxies?

### 2. Elements missing or invisible after export or import

**Symptoms.**
- (ja) "IFCに書き出したらモデルの半分が消えていた。ただのソリッド、階に紐づかないレイヤ、非表示要素が原因" (half the model vanished on IFC export; plain solids, layers not linked to stories and hidden elements were the cause) [src](https://note.com/toshio_nakamura/n/nc8db42ce3191)
- (zh) "revit导出ifc没有东西" (Revit IFC export produces nothing) [src](https://blog.csdn.net/d001014/article/details/140438872); "Revit导出IFC后缺图元和钢结构连接" (elements and steel connections missing after export) [src](https://www.autodesk.com.cn/support/technical/article/caas/sfdcarticles/sfdcarticles/CHS/Missing-elements-or-steel-connections-in-IFC-file-exported-from-Revit.html); "Revit打开IFC元素丢失或变形怎么办" (elements lost or deformed on opening IFC in Revit) [src](https://ask.csdn.net/questions/8391346)
- (ru) "через IFC конструкции из Теклы в Ревит не передаются нормально, при открытии в Ревите элементы теряются" (structures from Tekla do not transfer properly through IFC, elements are lost when opened in Revit) [src](https://forum.dwg.ru/showthread.php?t=146029); the later thread says Revit shows only the Tekla axes until a phase is assigned [src](https://forum.dwg.ru/showthread.php?t=146029)
- (en) "Some geometry is not shown in the viewer" with the answer "all IfcShapeRepresentations point to the 2D Plan context, which the engine skips" [src](https://github.com/xBimTeam/XbimGeometry/issues/547)
- (en) "Linking an MEP IFC drops many elements in Revit 2023; the log is full of validation errors." [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/658)
- (es) "al abrir un archivo IFC (2x3) se pierde parte de la información geométrica" (opening an IFC2x3 loses part of the geometry; Bonsai Viewer vs Bonsai in Blender) [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/8131)

**Why.** Export side: only visible elements of the active view are exported, categories not mapped fall to proxy or are skipped, linked models need their own export, 2D elements and annotations are separate, elements with no 3D `Body` are not shapes. Import side: Revit visibility, phase filter, category filters and DirectShape validation. Data side: geometry stored only in a 2D "Plan" context, unsupported entity (viewer logs "unexpected mesh type"), elements without containment or hidden in an "Unsorted" bucket [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/9372).

**Fix.** Compare element counts by IFC class before and after; list elements with no `Representation`, with only non-Body representations, or contained nowhere; on the Revit side check phase, visibility and view range.

**Assistant:** classify each "missing" element: (a) not in the IFC, (b) in the IFC without shape, (c) with shape in an unsupported or 2D context, (d) with shape but not contained, (e) hidden by the viewer/Revit settings. **Ask:** Which software exported it and with which settings (visible in view only? linked files? 2D?) Which viewer shows the gap? Can you name one missing element (type, level) so I can find it by name or GlobalId?

### 3. Elements shifted or deformed by a specific exporter or importer version

**Symptoms.** "Spaces moved outside the building and wrong heights after exporter update; only build 26.1.0.23 avoids shifting" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/924); "Horizontal displacement of some rooms" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/923); "Exported Revit to IFC4 and one wall is dislocated; I struggle with this for a year" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/424) (known bug for voided non-extruded walls in the IFC4 reference view; IFC2x3 as workaround); "Sloped floor footprint is generated around the project base point so the bounding box becomes huge" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/1004); "Closed square profiles (hollow sections) displaced in IFC2x3 and corrupt in IFC4" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/1010); "Beam openings get displaced in IFC4 along Z" (fixed in Revit 2026, no backport) [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/708).

**Why.** Open-source exporter builds change between releases; IFC2x3 and IFC4 use different code paths; some bugs only appear for a type of element (sloped floors, voided walls, hollow sections).

**Fix.** Re-export with the other IFC version, with the previous or newest build, or switch the problem element's export setting; give the maintainers a minimal Revit sample.

**Assistant:** compare each suspicious element with its host (bounding box inside host? opening within wall bounds?), report offsets in model units and metres, and list which elements deviate. Do not assert which build is good; the evidence is anecdotal. **Ask:** Exporter/add-in build and Revit version? IFC2x3 or IFC4? Did it work in an earlier export of the same project?

### 4. Representation types the next tool cannot read

**Symptoms.** "Revit emits IfcAdvancedBrep where extrusions would do and Solibri and Trimble cannot read it; I want an option to prevent it." [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/480); "Can't import IfcTriangulatedFaceSet" (Open IFC only partially supports IFC4; Link works for reference) [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/587); (ru) "при выгрузке в Navisworks SweptSolid отображается некорректно, нужна Tessellation" (exported to Navisworks, SweptSolid shows wrongly, tessellation needed) [src](https://rengabim.com/stati/prohodim-gosekspertizu-informacionnoj-modeli-pravilno/); "wall geometry is one combined solid with no data beyond the IFC class" [src](https://forum.dynamobim.com/t/automating-wall-extraction-from-ifc/115089); (ru) parametrics are lost when exporting from Revit/ArchiCAD; Bonsai stores the recipe in `BBIM_` property sets [src](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1063062/); (ja) "IfcIShapeProfileDefのFlangeSlopeは正の値でどっち向き？" (which direction is a positive FlangeSlope?) [src](https://zenn.dev/kiyuka/scraps/2894037aa4f997).

**Why.** See the comparison table in [06 doc, section 1.5](../../01_ifc_basics/06_geometry_placement_coordinates_units.md): extrusion is small and editable, tessellation is robust to display but loses intent, advanced brep needs more capable readers. A model view definition decides which are allowed. Revit's Open IFC and Link IFC paths read different subsets.

**Fix.** Choose the representation for the purpose (viewing: tessellated; handover: parametric where possible), re-export with another view definition, or link instead of open.

**Assistant:** report representation types per class (counts of `SweptSolid`, `Tessellation`, `Brep`, `AdvancedBrep`, `MappedRepresentation`, `CSG`), pick the `Body` representation by identifier (not "first in the list"), and relate the result to the target tool the user names. **Ask:** Where will the file be used (Navisworks, Solibri, Revit link, QTO)? Do you need editable dimensions or just display?

### 5. Heavy geometry: giant files, freezes, hangs

**Symptoms.** "Downloaded sample IFCs; the bigger ones freeze Bonsai with no error, BIMcollab opens them" and the cause found: "one handwash basin held 50% of all polygons" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/7632); "Opinion about high polygon count: manufacturer geometry with engraved logos" [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/4699); "95 MB IFC4 hangs; two waffle slabs with many coplanar faces" [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/2097); "764 MB IFC always runs out of memory (IfcFaceBasedSurfaceModel proxies)" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/6026); "industrial plant export 6h16 and 720 MB with insulation vs 2h43 without" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/892).

**Why.** Triangle count, many voids per element and complex booleans, not file size alone; manufacturer content; export settings (level of detail, 2D elements, insulation).

**Fix.** Rank elements by triangle count and class; exclude or simplify the heavy ones; Revit export Level of Detail tab; export visible view; remove 2D.

**Assistant:** report top-N elements by face count with class and name, share of total, and suggest what to exclude; if asked to produce a reduced copy, write a *new* file and report what was removed. **Ask:** What is the file for (coordination, model checking, FM)? What can be dropped (furniture, 2D, fasteners)?

### 6. Viewers and geometry kernels disagree

**Symptoms.** "Some exterior walls are not visible when exported in IFC 2x3, visible with IFC 4 and in other viewers" [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/451); "Incorrect boolean subtraction creates triangular surfaces across wall openings" [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/2269) (cause analysed: cutters 0.02-0.14 mm off straddle the coplanarity tolerance [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/2269)); "Wall Missing in Bonsai Model View compared to other viewers" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/8026); (ja) a 13-version comparison of web-ifc on the same IFC [src](https://zenn.dev/grok_prtex/articles/webifc-version-compare); (zh) "xbim 5.1加载SampleHouse4.ifc少了几面墙，5.0以前没问题" (walls missing in xBIM 5.1, fine before) [src](https://ask.csdn.net/questions/3915684).

**Why.** Three kernels (OpenCASCADE, CGAL, Manifold) and several engines (web-ifc, xBIM, IfcOpenShell) implement booleans with different tolerances and support different entities. Version upgrades change results.

**Fix.** Cross-check in a second tool; validate the IFC; find the minimal element; update; share a sample with maintainers.

**Assistant:** say "viewer-dependent" when the same element renders in another engine and the file validates; list the entity types involved; do not delete or "repair" geometry because one viewer fails. **Ask:** Which viewer and version? Does a second viewer show the element? Can you share the one element or storey?

### 7. Invalid geometry or placement data

**Symptoms.** "Error importing IFC: IfcLocalPlacement.RelativePlacement is None" (maintainer: the model is invalid, a placement lacks RelativePlacement) [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/6655); "IfcAxis2Placement3D has no attribute PlacementRelTo" (a placement pointing to an axis placement) [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/1622/blenderbim-import-error); "all IFC viewers crash on one IFC4X1 file; suspect a cyclic placement chain" [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/4523); "IfcShapeRepresentation.ContextOfItems is required, IFC0000104" [src](https://github.com/noddlesskcho/IFC-Repair-Studio); "IfcClosedShell.CfsFaces must not be empty" [src](https://github.com/noddlesskcho/IFC-Repair-Studio).

**Fix and assistant behaviour.** Run schema and rule validation first; report the entity ids and rule codes; propose a repair at the source and, if asked, a repaired copy; never claim the model is "fine" because it opens in one viewer.

### 8. Curtain walls

**Symptoms.** "Since newer exporter builds mullions (and about 20 percent of objects) are missing from IFC2x3 CV2 exports" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/944) (tracked internally; reported fixed in the 2027 line, no backport announced [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/944)); "'Ghost' mullions show up in the IFC that are not in the Revit model" (overlapping duplicate curtain walls) [src](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/revit-architecture-forum/quot-ghost-quot-mullions-in-ifc-export/td-p/13056918); "Curtain wall panels are hidden deep in the IFC hierarchy" [src](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/revit-architecture-forum/advanced-ifc-export-of-curtain-wall-revit-2018/td-p/8590346); "How to export a curtain wall as a single object?" [src](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/revit-architecture-forum/export-curtain-wall-to-ifc-as-a-single-object/td-p/12907563); "Curtain walls do not obey IfcExportAs" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/171); "Hundreds of IfcBuildingElementProxyType auto-generated" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/676); "IFC4 export of curtain walls crashes with AccessViolationException" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/992); "Glass partition with mullions and a door: IfcCurtainWall or IfcWall?" [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/6609) (answer: IfcWall, the building context decides).

**Why.** The Revit exporter hard-codes the mapping (container `IfcCurtainWall`, mullions `IfcMember`, panels `IfcPlate`), so `IfcExportAs` has no effect; the result is an aggregate whose parts must be followed through `IfcRelAggregates` to count them. Version bugs drop parts. The FM Handover MVD for COBie excludes plate and member [src](https://github.com/opensourceBIM/COBie-plugins/issues/10).

**Fix.** Remove overlapping duplicates; use the newest exporter; check IFC2x3 vs IFC4; classify partitions as walls if appropriate.

**Assistant:** count panels (`IfcPlate`) and mullions (`IfcMember`) per `IfcCurtainWall`, list curtain walls with zero members or zero panels with their Revit type name, and state whether the parts hang under the wall by aggregation. **Ask:** Which IFC version and view? Should the whole wall be one object (count as 1) or all parts?

### 9. Penetrations, sleeves and ProvisionForVoid

**Symptoms (real).** German practice: Revit void families exported as proxy/generic objects, not real openings; the bSI forum thread "ProvisionForVoid" describes a real project using a proxy plus property sets [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/provisionforvoid/1279); "Provision for Voids in IFC4x3 RC1" asks what replaces the proxy convention [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/provision-for-voids-in-ifc4x3-rc1/2998); "What is the right IFC entity for water outlet into a structural IFC model?" [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/what-is-the-right-ifc-entity-for-water-outlet-into-a-structural-ifc-model/1410); "Wall-based IfcOpeningElements from doors and windows behaving differently" (Solibri) [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/621); feature request for parametric MEP penetration openings [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/8037). In the synthetic data this is the most requested workflow (about 19 prompts in de, en, zh, ru, es, pt, fr, ja, ko, it): "pipes through walls without an opening", "sleeve list", "compare opening requests with structural openings".

**Why.** The architect/structure model holds real openings; the MEP model holds *requests* (proxies with a ProvisionForVoid type) or just pipes. Matching them needs geometry: intersect the MEP segment with the host, subtract existing openings, compare sizes with a tolerance.

**Assistant:** state the convention in use; never treat a request proxy as a real opening; produce a list with size, host, storey, GlobalIds; for BCF export confirm viewpoint and tolerance. **Ask:** What is the clearance rule (offset)? Which models hold the requests and which the openings? Fire-rated hosts only? Threshold size?

### 10. Quantities that ignore openings or use the wrong geometry

**Symptoms.** "GrossSideArea == NetSideArea in Bonsai QTO" for Revit walls with windows [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/9235) (analysis: the tool only checks `IfcRelVoidsElement`, so openings baked into the mesh are missed); "Qto_DoorBaseQuantities Width is right if width greater than height, otherwise reports the height" [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/comment/28629#Comment_28629); "Base quantities wrong for concrete columns with openings" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/644); "side-area quantities depend on wall orientation" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/9237); "get_volume loses precision far from the origin" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/9770).

**Assistant:** state the method (taken from `Qto_*` properties or computed from geometry), compare gross and net with the opening list, and flag walls where openings exist but gross equals net. **Ask:** Which quantity definition (net/gross, with or without openings) does your estimate need?

### 11. Room and space geometry

**Symptoms.** "Areas are exported as IfcSpace with a -3 m offset in IFC4" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/851); "Room geometry export broken: any room bounding object defining top or bottom is no longer used" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/891); (ru) zones with imperfect boundaries compute area in Revit but fail IFC export as a body [src](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1089018/); "areas without geometry" list in a repair tool [src](https://github.com/noddlesskcho/IFC-Repair-Studio).

**Assistant:** list `IfcSpace` with no representation, zero area or zero height; compare space bounding boxes with their storey and walls; report offsets. **Ask:** Rooms or Areas? Which boundary option (2D vs 3D room volume)?

### 12. Stairs and railings

Evidence is thin (8 real records). **Symptoms.** "Floors and stairs vanish when Export to IFC is 'By Type'" (set Export to IFC = Yes) [src](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/revit-architecture-forum/revit-2023-ifc-export-issue-floors-stair-was-not-exported-export/td-p/11530572); "IfcStair predefined type does not export" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/677); "inconsistent combination of stairs and railings in IFC2x3 export" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/715); "stair flights missing riser count, tread length and riser height; can it be filled from geometry?" [src](https://github.com/noddlesskcho/ifc-sg-site-object-repair) (answer: derive for straight flights, flag curved/spiral for manual review); "model stair space like other floors (wrong volume) or clip by the stair?" [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/ifcspace-and-ifcstair/1451); MCP side: "No stair tool; send_code_to_revit cannot satisfy StairsEditScope.Commit" [src](https://github.com/bimwright/rvt-mcp/issues/14).

**Why.** A stair is an assembly of flights, landings and railings; Revit stairs and railings need an explicit class mapping (see [export reference](../../03_software/revit/ifc_export_reference.md) section 5); creating stairs through the Revit API needs an edit scope that can fail with error-severity warnings.

**Assistant:** list `IfcStair` with predefined type, flights, landings and railings by aggregation; derive riser/tread only for straight runs and say so; for permit checks (riser height, tread width) ask the user for the thresholds, never use remembered national values; for creating stairs through a Revit tool, say whether the tool supports it and ask for confirmation before running arbitrary code. **Ask:** Which code/threshold? Straight or curved stairs? Should values be written to the model?

### 13. Duplicates, overlaps and clash false positives

**Symptoms.** "Wants clash sets, duplicate or overlapping elements (same class, near-identical geometry, duplicate GlobalId)" [src](https://github.com/OpenAEC-Foundation/OpenAEC-BIM-validator/issues/12); "ghost mullions" from overlapping walls [src](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/revit-architecture-forum/quot-ghost-quot-mullions-in-ifc-export/td-p/13056918); "multiple IfcOpeningElement at the same location" [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/711); "two walls overlap by 50 mm but no clash found" [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/4594).

**Assistant:** distinguish duplicate GlobalId, duplicate geometry (class + position + bounding box within tolerance) and overlapping-but-different; ask for the tolerance; list only, do not delete.

### 14. IFC elements in Revit cannot be edited or converted

**Symptoms.** (ja) "IFC由来の要素をMEP要素に変換するアドインを作ろうとしたが、編集不可だったりパラメータが設定できず断念した" (tried to build an add-in converting IFC elements to MEP elements; could not edit or set parameters) [src](https://qiita.com/KengoTanaka-BIM/items/6cec74e04bf581b21cf5); (ru) "IFC из ifcopenshell в Revit не становится семейством" (IFC from ifcopenshell opens in Revit as a wall but not a native family) [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/777); (zh) "链接IFC进Revit后好几个构件显示不对" (several elements show wrongly after linking IFC) [src](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/375018823).

**Why.** Link IFC creates DirectShape elements; there is no official one-click conversion to native families (see [import reference](../../03_software/revit/ifc_import_link_reference.md) section 7).

**Assistant:** explain that a linked IFC is an underlay; offer a model-over workflow or a script that creates generic geometry; do not promise parameters or editing.

## Edge cases and traps for MCP testing in this area

1. **Hole is not in the wall body.** Reading the wall's `Body` and concluding "no opening" is wrong; always inspect `IfcRelVoidsElement`. The reverse is also true: an opening object may exist but the viewer may skip it (void limit). Test with a file where the relationship is missing and with one where it is present.
2. **Invalid openings.** An `IfcOpeningElement` not related to any product is invalid; `IfcRelVoidsElement` needs an `IfcElement` (a bridge part or storey cannot be voided) [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/4425). A tool should report these as file errors, not hide them.
3. **Fills vs voids direction.** Many people mix up which side is inverse (`HasOpenings` on the host, `HasFillings` on the opening). A prompt like "for each wall give HasOpenings -> opening -> filling" is a good probe.
4. **Provision proxies are not openings.** A proxy of type PROVISIONFORVOID is a request. A tool must not count it as a hole nor remove it. IFC4.3 discussions about what replaces it are unresolved [src](https://forums.buildingsmart.org/t/provision-for-voids-in-ifc4x3-rc1/2998).
5. **Void limits and tolerances.** "Opening Cuts Skipped" is a viewer-side limit, not a model fault; tolerance of 1e-4 in coplanarity checks can leave slivers when cutters are 0.02-0.14 mm off [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/2269). A diagnostic tool should say "geometry kernel issue" only after validating the file.
6. **`Representation[0]` is not the body.** Select by `RepresentationIdentifier = Body`; some files only have a `Plan` context representation [src](https://github.com/xBimTeam/XbimGeometry/issues/547). Mapped items carry two transforms (`MappingOrigin` and `MappingTarget`); a tool that reads only the placement reports 0,0,0 [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/6277).
7. **Type products carry maps.** Geometry may sit on `IfcTypeProduct.RepresentationMaps`, not on the occurrence; an iterator that skips types looks empty [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/2242).
8. **World vs local coordinates.** Bounding boxes and dimension extraction differ if the wall is rotated or the site is far from the origin; ask whether world coordinates are wanted [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/discussions/8102). See the coordinates doc for precision traps.
9. **Open shells.** Volume of a non-closed shell is meaningless; a good tool warns and lists non-manifold items [src](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/1307/detect-and-fix-objects-where-the-shell-is-not-closed).
10. **Heavy-file handling.** Do not load 800 MB of geometry blindly. Offer a sampled or per-class pass, report time and memory, and avoid a hang (the quadratic boolean cases are real [src](https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc/issues/2097)).
11. **Silent drops.** A converter that "succeeds" with fewer elements than the source (4000 in, 3900 out) is a classic trap; compare counts by class and name the missing ones.
12. **Writes.** Adding an opening needs the opening, `IfcRelVoidsElement`, placement relative to the host, and (for doors/windows) the fills relation, created in the right order and idempotent on retry (no duplicates). Creating the door before the opening is a documented beginner mistake [src](https://zenn.dev/toggle_inc/articles/cf8df02f7b4ba7).
13. **Revit link/open limits.** IFC elements imported by Link are DirectShapes; asking an assistant to "change the wall thickness of the linked IFC wall" has no valid result. It should explain and offer an alternative.
14. **Revit exporter versions.** Several behaviours depend on the add-in build (curtain walls, rooms, beam openings). An assistant should ask for the version rather than state a universal rule, and should label statements as "reported by users".
15. **Curtain wall counting.** Count depends on whether parts are aggregated, split, or exported as separate proxies; ask whether one curtain wall is one object or its parts. IFC2x3 and IFC4 differ. Do not suggest changing `IfcExportAs` on curtain walls as a fix; the mapping is fixed in the exporter [src](https://github.com/Autodesk/revit-ifc/issues/171).
16. **Stairs.** Riser and tread from geometry are reliable only for straight runs; spiral and curved stairs need human review. Never supply a regulatory limit from memory; the code check must use values the user gives.
17. **Revit MCP transactions.** Stair creation through arbitrary code can run into edit-scope failures and long hangs [src](https://github.com/bimwright/rvt-mcp/issues/14). Test timeouts, rollback and a clear message that the stair was or was not created.
18. **Duplicates.** Distinguish repeated GlobalIds, duplicate geometry and legitimate overlaps (joined walls, embedded elements); require a tolerance and never delete automatically.
19. **Clash points.** In IfcClash the two points of a clash are tied to the piercing edges of each element, not to the bounding box corners, and can change with pair order [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/6138). A tool that explains clash locations should say so.
20. **Infrastructure geometry.** Alignment-based solids and surfaces (`IfcSectionedSolidHorizontal`, gradient curves, linear placement) are only partly supported by several kernels [src](https://github.com/IfcOpenShell/IfcOpenShell/issues/3098); an empty shape does not mean the model has no geometry.
21. **Language.** Reply in the user's language but keep IFC names, GlobalIds and numbers unchanged; users mix vocabularies ("Durchbruch/Aussparung/Öffnung", "проём/отверстие", "开洞/预留洞").
